Privacy Policy

VibeScale – Indian Edition
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VibeScale – Indian Edition (the “App”) handles information when you use the App. The App is a casual party and team game.

1. Information the App handles

The App is designed to work locally on your device. Game-related information such as preferences, game progress, previously seen items, rounds and scores may be stored on your device using Android's local app storage so that the App can retain your experience between sessions.

The App does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or email address to play.

2. How information is used

Locally stored game information is used to operate the game, remember settings and support gameplay. The App does not use this information to build advertising profiles.

3. Sharing and sale of information

The App's local game data is not transmitted by the App to a developer-operated server. The developer does not sell personal information collected through the App.

4. Internet, analytics and advertising

The App is intended to provide its gameplay experience on-device. This policy does not describe third-party analytics or advertising services because none are intentionally part of the App's stated functionality. If a future version introduces online services, analytics, advertising or other data collection, this policy will be updated before or alongside that change.

5. Permissions and device features

The App may use your device's vibration feature to provide gameplay feedback. Vibration is used for the game experience and is not used to identify you.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience casual game and does not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information in connection with the App may contact the developer using the details below.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game information stored locally remains on your device until it is overwritten, cleared from the App, or removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage, subject to Android device behaviour and backups configured by you.

8. Security

The App relies on Android's application sandbox and local storage mechanisms for on-device game data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure; please keep your device and operating system updated.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. The latest version will be published at this page, with its effective date revised when material changes are made.
